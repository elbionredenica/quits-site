Quits

Split costs. Stay friends.

Quits keeps track of who paid for what with friends, roommates and trips, and tells everyone the fewest payments that settle it.

Need help? Email elbionredenica04@icloud.com. Say what you tried and what happened, and you'll hear back within a few days.

Getting started

  • Sign in with Apple. It's the only way in, so there's no password to remember. You can hide your email from Apple's sheet.
  • Log a cost in one sentence. Type or say something like “Dinner 84 with Sam and Mia” and Quits fills in the amount, the people and the split. Check it, then tap Add.
  • Add friends who aren't on Quits yet by name. They can claim their spot later with an invite code, link or QR code.

Questions

How do invites work?

Open a group, tap Invite, and share the code (it looks like K7QM4-XHT2R). Your friend opens Quits, taps Join with a code, and picks their name if you already added them. Codes last 30 days.

How do I settle up?

Open a group or a person and tap Settle up. Quits shows the fewest payments that clear every balance. Paying happens outside Quits; record it there so the balance updates for everyone.

Someone changed an expense. Who was it?

Everyone in a group can add, edit and delete its expenses. Activity shows each change with the name of the person who made it, and a deleted expense can be restored.

Siri, widgets and Control Center

Say “Log an expense in Quits” or “How much do I owe in Quits.” Add the Quits widget to your Home or Lock Screen to see your balance, and the Log expense control to Control Center or the Action button to start talking right away. Siri asks you to unlock your iPhone first.

How do I delete my account?

In Quits, tap your avatar, then Account, then Delete account. Groups only you are in are deleted. In groups you share, your expenses stay under your name so everyone's balances still add up, but your account and email are removed. This can't be undone.

What happens to my data?

Read the privacy policy. In short: no ads, no tracking, and voice is turned into text on your iPhone.
