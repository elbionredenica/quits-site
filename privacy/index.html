Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026.

This policy explains how Quits (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares information when you use the Quits app, and the choices you have. Quits is made by Elbion Redenica.

In short: we keep what you need to split costs with the people you choose, and nothing else. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Voice and the sentences you type are understood on your iPhone.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email address Apple shares, and we create a user ID for your account. Apple lets you hide your email address; then we only see an address Apple relays to you. Your profile holds the name you choose, your default currency and your avatar color.
  • Groups and people. The groups you create or join, and the names of people you add who aren't on Quits yet (and their email address, if you choose to add one).
  • Expenses and payments. What each expense was for, the amount and currency, the date, who paid, how it's split, notes, comments, and the payments you record to settle up.
  • Receipt photos. A photo you attach to an expense. Photos are only uploaded when you attach them.
  • Invite codes. The codes you create to invite people to a group.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report a comment, an expense or a person, we keep the report, who sent it and a copy of what was reported, so we can review it. When you block someone, we keep that so their comments stay hidden from you.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Voice. When you log by voice, your iPhone turns speech into text on the device. The audio is never sent to us.
  • Reading sentences. Turning “Dinner 84 with Sam” into an expense happens on your iPhone, with rules built into Quits and, when they're unsure, Apple's on-device model. Only the expense you save is sent to us.
  • Camera. Scanning an invite QR code happens on your iPhone. Receipts are covered above.
  • Widgets and Siri. Your balance is saved in a small file on your iPhone so widgets and Siri can show it. It's deleted when you sign out.

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, your location, your contacts, health information or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

  • To run Quits: your account, your groups, balances, settling up, invites, widgets and Siri.
  • To keep everyone in a group in sync across their devices.
  • To keep Quits safe and prevent abuse.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not show ads, do not use your information for advertising, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we share information

  • People in your groups. Everyone in a group sees the names and avatars of the other members, and everything added to that group: expenses, payments, comments, receipt photos, balances and activity. People you aren't in a group with can't see any of it.
  • Service providers. Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage, on servers in the United States. It processes information only to provide its service to us. Apple provides Sign in with Apple.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Quits.
  • Business transfers. If Quits is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile at any time in the app: your avatar, then Account.
  • Delete your account at any time in the app: your avatar, then Account, then Delete account.
  • Camera, microphone and speech recognition can be turned off in iOS Settings.
  • Access, correction and copies. To get a copy of your information or correct it, email elbionredenica04@icloud.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete it together with your profile, and the groups only you were in, with their expenses and receipt photos.

Groups you share with other people belong to them too, so they stay. In those groups, what you added stays under your name so everyone's balances still add up, but it is no longer linked to an account, and your email address is removed. Your name stays on those expenses for the people in the group.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Our database only lets people see groups they belong to, and access to it is limited to what running Quits requires. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you use Quits from outside the United States, your information is transferred to the United States. Where the law requires, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in our provider's data processing terms.

Children

Quits is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it.

Additional information for EU and UK users

We process your information to perform our contract with you (running your account and the features you use), for our legitimate interests (security and preventing abuse), with your consent (camera, microphone and speech recognition, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings), and to meet legal obligations. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your information, and to receive it in a portable format. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Additional information for California residents

We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined under California law, and do not use it for targeted advertising. You may request to know, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and we will not discriminate against you for making a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top, and we will tell you in the app about material changes.
